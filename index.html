▸🎮 How to play

Move & aim
  • WASD move, mouse to look
  • Click to shoot · Space jump
  • 1 Rifle · 2 Pistol · 3 Shotgun · 4 DMR · 5 Burst · R reload · G grenade 🧨 · ZXVBNM quick chat
  • Headshots do double damage — aim high!
  • Tab scoreboard · T taunt 😜
Win the match
  • First to 25 kills wins, or most kills when the round timer ends (pick 3, 5 or 10 minutes in Controls & Map)
  • 🧨 Grenades: 2 per life (refilled when you respawn) — press G or the 🧨 button. They arc, pop on impact, and hurt every enemy nearby (90 damage at the centre). No self-damage, no friendly fire
  • 🌉 Bridges & sniper towers: every map has a bridge and two gold sniper towers — just walk up the steps, no jumping needed. High ground sees the whole arena
  • 🔫 New guns (free for everyone): DMR (big damage, very precise, key 4) and Burst rifle (3 pellets per shot, key 5). The shop SMG, Sniper and LMG still cost coins
  • Kill streaks (3, 5, 8…) get announced to everyone
  • You respawn 3 seconds after being cubed
  • 💀 Kill cam: for those 3 seconds the camera shows you the fighter who got you — so you always know who shot you
  • Spawn protection: for 3 seconds after (re)spawning you cannot take damage — a 🛡️ shield shows on you. Shooting ends it early, so hold fire if you want the full 3s
  • On mobile: hold your phone sideways (landscape) — left joystick to move, drag on the right side to aim. Shoot with tap-to-shoot or switch to the 🔥 FIRE button in the Controls & Map card on the main menu (or in Pause). You can also move the buttons — ✏️ Move Buttons in the same card, or Edit Layout in Pause
  • TDM: first team to 25 kills wins — Alpha (blue) vs Bravo (pink), bots split across both teams
  • Spawn protection: 3s of no damage after (re)spawning — 🛡️ shield shows on you. Shooting ends it early
  • Account: sign up with your fighter name + email + password in the My Account card and your name, level and match history follow you to any device. Log in needs only email + password — your name is fetched from your account. Without an account, history stays on this device only
  • Premium Shop: earn 🪙 coins in matches (10 per kill + 50 win bonus + 20 per match) and spend them on weapon skins and new guns (SMG, Sniper, LMG). Coins only — no real money, no checkout
  • Pause / return: press Esc or P (or the ⏸ button) to pause — Resume, or Main Menu to quit to the menu. Your account stays logged in
Online rooms — the honest bits
  • Rooms hold 2–4 players and connect directly between browsers (PeerJS) — free, no server cost
  • If the host closes the game, the match ends for everyone. Guests leaving just drop out
  • Everyone needs the game open in their browser — share the room code, or better: send the invite link from the lobby. Anyone who opens it lands straight in your lobby automatically, no code to type
Quick Match & real matchmaking — the truth
  • Quick Match first searches for real players online (about 25 seconds), then bots fill the match to 10 players (e.g. 2 real + 8 bots). Host-authoritative: only the host simulates bots (movement, shooting, damage) and broadcasts bot state to everyone via PeerJS — all devices see the same bots. Bots are always labelled BOT — never fake "real" strangers
  • 🩹 Healing: press H (or tap the 🩹 button / the heal bar) to use a medkit — it takes 3 seconds and restores +50 HP. You get 2 medkits per match. Healing is interrupted if you take damage or shoot — the medkit is only used up when healing finishes. Bots get the same deal: 2 medkits each, they heal when hurt and far from a fight, and damage interrupts them too — no unfair advantage either way
  • True random matchmaking with strangers needs a dedicated game server running 24/7 plus many players online at the same time
  • Vercel is static hosting only — it serves this page but cannot run a game server, so it cannot match strangers
  • For now, the room-code mode above is the free way to play with real friends
  • Chat is player-to-player: tap the 💬 icon for Quick Chat presets or type your own message (Team / All in TDM). Messages in online rooms travel browser-to-browser via PeerJS — there is no chat server and nothing is stored. A simple filter replaces common abusive words with ***. In bot matches your typed messages show locally; bots never reply to free text, they only drop occasional quick-chat lines
About earning: hosting this game (for example on Vercel) is free but pays nothing by itself. Games earn money when they are published on game portals like CrazyGames or itch.io, where the portal runs ads and shares the revenue with the developer. That is the real route for this game — there are no ads or payouts inside this page.
