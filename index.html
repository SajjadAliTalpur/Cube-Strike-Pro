🎮 How to play

Move & aim
  • WASD move, mouse to look
  • Click to shoot · Space jump
  • 1 Rifle · 2 Pistol · 3 Shotgun · R reload
  • Headshots do double damage — aim high!
  • Tab scoreboard · T taunt 😜
Win the match
  • First to 25 kills wins, or most kills when the 5-minute timer ends
  • Kill streaks (3, 5, 8…) get announced to everyone
  • You respawn 3 seconds after being cubed
  • Spawn protection: for 3 seconds after (re)spawning you cannot take damage — a 🛡️ shield shows on you. Shooting ends it early, so hold fire if you want the full 3s
  • On mobile: hold your phone sideways (landscape) — left joystick to move, drag on the right side to aim, tap the screen to shoot (no FIRE button). Weapon, reload, jump and crouch are icon buttons in the transparent bar at the bottom
  • TDM: first team to 25 kills wins — Alpha (blue) vs Bravo (pink), bots split across both teams
  • Spawn protection: 3s of no damage after (re)spawning — 🛡️ shield shows on you. Shooting ends it early
  • Account: sign up (email + password) in the My Account card and your name, level and match history follow you to any device. Without an account, history stays on this device only
  • Premium Shop: earn 🪙 coins in matches (10 per kill + 50 win bonus + 20 per match) and spend them on weapon skins and new guns (SMG, Sniper, LMG). Real-money coin purchase is coming soon — no checkout here yet
  • Donate: the ❤️ Donate button opens the owner's donation link — the owner sets their own link in the DONATE_URL setting; no payment details are built into the game
  • Pause / return: press Esc or P (or the ⏸ button) to pause — Resume, or Main Menu to quit to the menu. Your account stays logged in
Online rooms — the honest bits
  • Rooms hold 2–4 players and connect directly between browsers (PeerJS) — free, no server cost
  • If the host closes the game, the match ends for everyone. Guests leaving just drop out
  • Everyone needs the game open in their browser — share the room code, or better: send the invite link from the lobby. Anyone who opens it lands straight in your lobby automatically, no code to type
Quick Match & real matchmaking — the truth
  • Quick Match fills a 10-player lobby fast. Until real players are online, empty slots are filled with bots, always labelled BOT — never fake "real" strangers
  • True random matchmaking with strangers needs a dedicated game server running 24/7 plus many players online at the same time
  • Vercel is static hosting only — it serves this page but cannot run a game server, so it cannot match strangers
  • For now, the room-code mode above is the free way to play with real friends
  • Chat is player-to-player: tap the 💬 icon for Quick Chat presets or type your own message (Team / All in TDM). Messages in online rooms travel browser-to-browser via PeerJS — there is no chat server and nothing is stored. A simple filter replaces common abusive words with ***. In bot matches your typed messages show locally; bots never reply to free text, they only drop occasional quick-chat lines
About earning: hosting this game (for example on Vercel) is free but pays nothing by itself. Games earn money when they are published on game portals like CrazyGames or itch.io, where the portal runs ads and shares the revenue with the developer. That is the real route for this game — there are no ads or payouts inside this page.
